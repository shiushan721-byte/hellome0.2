# Timing and Terminology

## Timing Risks

Watch for:
- target language expansion
- fast original speech
- dense legal or technical text
- visible mouth movement

When timing is tight, prefer meaning compression over unnatural literalism.

## Terminology Protection

Always lock:
- product names
- company names
- feature names
- regulatory language
- metrics and numbers

If no glossary exists, create a temporary protected-term list and mark it for review.
