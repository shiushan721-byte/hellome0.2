# Localization Modes

## Subtitle-Only

Best for:
- fastest turnaround
- preserving original speaker authenticity

Risk:
- viewer still hears the original language

## Voiceover-Dub

Best for:
- explanatory or documentary-style pieces
- when full lip-sync is unnecessary

Risk:
- can feel detached if badly paced

## Full-Dub

Best for:
- polished localized deliverables
- when the target audience expects a native-language audio experience

Risk:
- timing pressure

## Lip-Sync Adaptation

Best for:
- presenter-led or face-forward content where mouth match matters

Risk:
- highest constraint and revision pressure

## Hybrid Localization

Best for:
- some languages need subtitles, others need dubbing
- mixed requirements across regions
