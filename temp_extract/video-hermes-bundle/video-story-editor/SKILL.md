# Video Story Editor

Shape material into a felt experience, not just an information sequence.

Use this skill when the core challenge is emotional progression, editorial rhythm, and narrative shape.

## Inputs

Expect:
- user goal
- audience
- source assets
- output spec
- style reference if any
- success definition

Useful inputs include:
- footage selects
- voiceover or script
- music direction
- reference mood pieces
- brand narrative context

## Workflow

1. Define the emotional arc.
2. Choose the story shape.
3. Build the beat map.
4. Assign visual and audio roles.
5. Return an editorial blueprint.

## Emotional Arc

Decide what the viewer should feel over time:
- curiosity to revelation
- tension to release
- skepticism to belief
- intimacy to conviction
- wonder to reflection

If the desired emotion is generic, sharpen it before editing structure.

## Story Shape

Choose the dominant structure:
- `hook-reveal`
- `build-payoff`
- `mood-drift`
- `thesis-proof`
- `question-answer`
- `fragment-montage`

Use [references/story-shapes.md](./references/story-shapes.md) for selection guidance.

## Beat Map

For each beat, define:
- emotional job
- visual job
- audio job
- transition logic
- whether the beat expands, pivots, or resolves

Detailed beat guidance lives in [references/beat-mapping.md](./references/beat-mapping.md).

## Visual and Audio Roles

Specify:
- what footage or imagery carries the emotional weight
- whether voiceover leads or supports
- whether music drives the pace or follows it
- where restraint is more powerful than additional cuts

Do not let every beat compete for climax. Hold back enough contrast for the strongest moment to matter.

## Failure and Escalation

Escalate when:
- the material is better suited to demo, speaker, or explainer treatment
- the footage cannot honestly support the claimed emotion
- the brief wants too many tonal directions at once
- there is no plausible audio plan for the desired mood

## Output

Return:
- selected story shape
- emotional arc
- beat map
- visual and audio plan
- pacing and risk notes
