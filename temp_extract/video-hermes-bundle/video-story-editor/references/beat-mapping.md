# Beat Mapping

## Beat Template

For each beat, record:
- beat name
- emotional purpose
- visual source
- audio source
- timing feel
- transition intention

## Rhythm Guidance

- vary hold lengths on purpose
- do not peak too early unless the format is intentionally disruptive
- use silence, breath, or visual stillness as rhythm tools
- avoid stacking music swell, text emphasis, and rapid cuts on every beat

## Common Failures

- every beat feels equally loud
- no emotional change across the piece
- footage selection is descriptive but not evocative
- the ending lands weaker than the opening
