# Story Shapes

## Hook-Reveal

Best for:
- curiosity-driven shorts
- trailers
- manifesto openings

Logic:
- open with tension or promise
- reveal the meaning later

## Build-Payoff

Best for:
- cinematic brand pieces
- proof that needs momentum

Logic:
- escalate steadily
- land on a clear high point

## Mood-Drift

Best for:
- atmospheric or reflective pieces
- artful montage work

Logic:
- feeling accumulates more than plot

## Thesis-Proof

Best for:
- persuasive storytelling
- founder or mission films

Logic:
- make a claim
- support it with layered evidence

## Question-Answer

Best for:
- explainer-adjacent emotional pieces

Logic:
- pose a tension
- answer it through progression

## Fragment-Montage

Best for:
- collage, manifesto, texture-led work

Logic:
- meaning emerges from juxtaposition
