# Visual Modes

## Diagram-Led

Best for:
- technical explainers
- systems thinking
- comparisons

Strength:
- clarity

Risk:
- can feel dry if pacing is flat

## Kinetic-Type-Led

Best for:
- quote-driven pieces
- manifesto energy
- bold short-form explainers

Strength:
- speed and emphasis

Risk:
- overload if every line is treated like a punchline

## Illustration-Led

Best for:
- friendly explainers
- brand storytelling
- abstract topics needing metaphor

Strength:
- warmth and flexibility

Risk:
- inconsistency if asset style drifts

## UI-Motion-Led

Best for:
- product explainers
- interface concept videos

Strength:
- perceived modernity and precision

Risk:
- can look generic without a clear system

## Generated-Visual-Led

Best for:
- concept-heavy sequences
- mood-first or speculative visuals

Strength:
- breadth of image possibilities

Risk:
- continuity problems unless constraints are defined
