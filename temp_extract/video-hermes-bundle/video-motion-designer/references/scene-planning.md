# Scene Planning

## Scene Template

For each scene, record:
- scene goal
- on-screen idea
- motion idea
- text requirement
- asset requirement
- transition in
- transition out
- build risk

## Sequence Logic

Check that the scenes as a set:
- progress logically
- do not repeat the same reveal mechanic too often
- reserve the strongest visual move for a high-value moment
- stay within the chosen visual mode
