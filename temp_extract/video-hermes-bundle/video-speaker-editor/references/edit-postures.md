# Edit Postures

## Authority-Clean

Best for:
- founders
- executives
- thought leadership

Rules:
- clean cuts
- minimal gimmicks
- captions support rather than dominate

## Creator-Punchy

Best for:
- short-form creators
- mobile-first social content

Rules:
- faster openings
- more visible text emphasis
- tighter pauses

## Interview-Natural

Best for:
- podcasts on camera
- testimonials
- conversational interviews

Rules:
- preserve rhythm
- avoid flattening personality
- support clarity without erasing spontaneity

## Sales-Conversion

Best for:
- offer videos
- product or service pitches

Rules:
- keep proof moments visible
- tighten logic
- respect CTA pacing

## Educational-Guided

Best for:
- explainers
- tutorials with a visible host

Rules:
- signpost structure
- let the viewer know what is coming next
- use reinforcement text carefully
