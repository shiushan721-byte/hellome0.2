# Subtitle and Framing

## Subtitle Rules

- use subtitles when comprehension, accessibility, or platform norms demand them
- do not let caption styling overwhelm trust-heavy footage
- emphasize only the words that carry meaning

## Framing Rules

- punch in only when it adds intent
- use reframing to restore focus, not to simulate false energy
- if the speaker's face carries emotion, keep it readable

## Overlay Guidance

Add support overlays only when they reduce confusion or strengthen proof. Avoid decorating every sentence with text.
