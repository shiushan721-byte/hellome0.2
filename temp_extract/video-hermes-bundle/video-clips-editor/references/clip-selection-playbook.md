# Clip Selection Playbook

## Goal

Find moments that can stand alone as short videos.

## Selection Tests

A strong candidate usually passes at least three:

- immediate hook
- single clear idea
- emotional charge
- useful insight
- quotable language
- clean standalone context
- feasible duration for the target platform

## Weak Candidate Warnings

Avoid clips that depend too heavily on missing context, long setup, private jokes, or visual information the viewer cannot see in the cut.

## Batch Mix

Do not make every clip feel identical. A strong batch usually mixes:
- one broad-reach hook
- one high-value insight
- one emotionally sticky moment
- one proof or example-driven moment

## Timestamp Guidance

When transcripts exist, prefer timestamp windows that can be tightened later.
When transcripts do not exist, mark approximate in/out ranges and note confidence.
