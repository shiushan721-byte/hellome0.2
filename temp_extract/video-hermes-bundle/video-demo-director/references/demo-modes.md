# Demo Modes

## Live Capture

Use when:
- the real product experience is part of the proof
- the UI changes dynamically
- realism is more persuasive than perfect pacing

Risks:
- unstable states
- distracting noise
- repeatability problems

## Synthetic Demo

Use when:
- the workflow is deterministic
- the content is terminal-heavy or step-driven
- you need precise pacing or narration sync

Risks:
- can feel less real if over-designed
- may miss the authenticity of the live product

## Hybrid Demo

Use when:
- some moments must be captured live
- some explanation requires overlays, inserts, or reconstructed views

Risks:
- style inconsistency
- too much support design overwhelming the product
