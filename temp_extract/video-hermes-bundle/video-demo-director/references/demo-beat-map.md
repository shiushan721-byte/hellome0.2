# Demo Beat Map

## Standard Shape

Use this shape unless the dispatch brief strongly suggests another:

1. Hook: what the viewer gets
2. Context: where we are and why it matters
3. First action: the quickest meaningful interaction
4. Core proof: the moment value becomes obvious
5. Secondary support: one or two reinforcing moments
6. Close: outcome, CTA, or next step

## Beat Notes

For each beat, record:
- duration target
- screen action
- narration role
- caption role
- emphasis treatment

## Common Failure Modes

- too much setup before value
- too many tiny interactions
- camera movement with no narrative reason
- explaining features instead of outcomes
