# Style Lenses

## Pacing

Check:
- average shot hold feeling
- whether openings are abrupt or gradual
- whether momentum builds linearly or in bursts

## Visual Grammar

Check:
- framing tightness
- movement feeling
- overlay density
- composition cleanliness versus chaos

## Story Shape

Check:
- whether the piece is hook-first, reveal-first, mood-first, or proof-first
- where the emotional peak lands

## Audio Intent

Check:
- whether music leads or supports
- whether silence is used strategically
- whether voice is intimate, authoritative, cinematic, or playful

## Transferability

For every observation, ask:
- is this style, or just this creator's subject matter?
- would this still work for the user's actual product or message?
