# Format Selection

## Short-Social

Best for:
- fast awareness
- repeated distribution
- one idea per video

## Product-Demo

Best for:
- showing how something works
- proving value through interaction

## Talking-Head

Best for:
- trust
- authority
- direct persuasion

## Cinematic-Montage

Best for:
- emotional positioning
- brand films
- mood-first storytelling

## Explainer-Animation

Best for:
- abstract topics
- systems and concepts
- when footage does not exist

## Hybrid-Showcase

Best for:
- combining proof and polish
- source footage plus designed support

## Selection Rule

Pick the format that best matches the goal and the strongest available truth source. Do not pick a format only because it sounds premium.
