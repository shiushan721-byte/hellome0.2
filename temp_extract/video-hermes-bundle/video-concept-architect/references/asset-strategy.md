# Asset Strategy

## Source-Led

Use when real footage or real events carry the proof.

## Speaker-Led

Use when the face, voice, and credibility of a person carry the video.

## Screen-Led

Use when interface or workflow is the main source of truth.

## Design-Led

Use when layout, typography, diagrams, and controlled motion must carry the explanation.

## Generated-Led

Use when visuals must be synthesized because no footage exists or concept range matters more than realism.

## Hybrid

Use when no single asset type is enough.

## Decision Rule

Choose the lightest asset strategy that can still honestly deliver the promise.
